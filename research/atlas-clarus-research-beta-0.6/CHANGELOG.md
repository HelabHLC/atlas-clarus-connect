# ATLAS Clarus Public Research Beta

## 0.6.0-beta.2 — 24 November 2026

- Public, standalone, data-free Colour Advisory and Spectral Lab.
- New official source catalogue with user-clicked CIE, freieFarbe and Fogra links.
- Direct import of official CIE 1931 observer CSV, individual D50/D55/D65/D75/A CSVs and the nine-column CIE LED CSV (B2/B3).
- Local selected-reference extraction from user-held HLC CxF/CGATS and FOGRA51 spectral CGATS, with CSV export.
- Strict 36-point constraints, provenance labels and rejection of unsupported >1 reflectance.
- DE/EN, local-only computation, reproducible JSON/CSV export and experimental-model warning.
- No third-party numerical tables distributed. No changes to the private scientific development repository.
