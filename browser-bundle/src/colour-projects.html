LOCAL PILOT

Your colours. Your decisions. A project you can take with you and keep developing.

Collect colour sets, record changes and choose the design versions you want to use. Everything runs in this browser. Download the project JSON as your portable backup.

1. Open your project

Or drop project.clarus.json here. Extract handover ZIPs first.

No project open.

2. Bring your colours

Use Colour handoff to prepare recorded originals from your active palette. Its decision IDs and complete source document travel into the project.

3. Save and share

Project JSON reopens the complete project. The ZIP adds a readable passport and separate working / chosen swatches with their provenance JSON. Colour Kit opens the palette JSON inside each set.

Keep source images separately. A chosen version records a design decision; it is not measured production approval or proof of authorship.

Checking the project workspace …

Edits keep the original decision and its history. Changing a working colour does not change an already chosen version.
