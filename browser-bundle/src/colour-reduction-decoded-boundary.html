For T-shirt printing: what this preparation means

For flat-colour motifs, a small palette can help you and the printer discuss the design. This RGB-only pilot creates no separations, screens, halftones, underbase, trapping or ink recipes. The printer determines those production steps.

Transparency and partial opacity are preserved. Semi-transparent edges can produce additional apparent colours on fabric; no garment background is baked in. Dithering is off. The number shown counts visible RGB motif colours, not inks or screens.

For photographic DTF, DTG or sublimation work, use the full-colour original unless you deliberately want a reduced look or the printer requests it. The preview is not a fabric proof or production approval.
